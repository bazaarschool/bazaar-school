T stands for the trade day. Under T+1, a trade done on Monday settles on Tuesday: the buyer receives the shares and the seller receives the money. India finished moving every listed share to T+1 in January 2023, which made it one of the fastest large markets in the world. SEBI has since allowed an optional same-day cycle for a short list of shares, but T+1 is the normal rule.

Until settlement your broking app may show new purchases as "T1" holdings. You already own them; the paperwork is catching up.

### Example

You sell 50 shares on Thursday. The money reaches your trading account on Friday, and you can withdraw it to your bank from then on.

### Watch out

Settlement counts working days, not calendar days. Sell on a Friday and the money usually arrives on Monday, later still if a market holiday falls in between.
