## How the course works

There are three levels: Basics, Intermediate and Advanced. Each level is split into modules of four to six short chapters, and each chapter is numbered, so 2.3 means the third chapter of the second module. Most chapters take ten minutes or less.

Every chapter ends the same way. First a short list of what you can now do. Then a three-question self-check: answer in your head, then tap to see ours. Then a button to mark the chapter as done, which fills the progress bars on the level pages and the curriculum.

Your progress is saved in this browser only. There is no account and nothing is sent anywhere, which also means a different phone or browser starts fresh.

Whenever a word in a chapter has a dotted underline, it opens a card in the [glossary](/glossary/). Each card is one idea with a worked example in rupees, and takes about two minutes.

Bazaar Stories and Bazaar AI sit outside the numbered path. Read them whenever you like.

## A rhythm that works

One chapter a day, the self-check before you close the tab, and a glossary card whenever a word trips you up. At that pace a module takes less than a week.

## What we will never do

We will never give you a stock tip, a price target or a "sure-shot" anything. We are not SEBI-registered advisers, and the course explains how markets work rather than what to buy. The [disclaimer](/disclaimer/) has the details.
