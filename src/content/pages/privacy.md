*This is a starter policy written for a static educational website. Review it with a professional before relying on it.*

## Who we are

Bazaar School ("we", "us") operates bazaarschool.com. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

## Information we collect

1. **Information you give us**, such as your email address when you subscribe to the Monday lesson or write to us.
2. **Usage data collected automatically**, only if you allow analytics cookies: pages visited, approximate location, device and browser type, and the page that referred you.

## Cookies

We use essential cookies and local storage to run the site and remember your choices (for example, light or dark mode and your cookie preferences). With your consent we also use analytics cookies (Google Analytics) and, if advertising is enabled, advertising cookies (Google AdSense). You can change your choice at any time using "Cookie preferences" in the footer. Google's own policies apply to data it collects; see [Google Ads Settings](https://www.google.com/settings/ads) to manage personalised ads.

## How we use your information

To deliver and improve the site, send the newsletter you asked for, answer your messages, measure what is read, and display advertising where enabled. We do not sell your personal information.

## Newsletter

Subscriptions are processed through our hosting provider's form service and our email provider. Every email includes an unsubscribe link. Unsubscribing removes you from the list.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, India under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, the EU or UK under GDPR, or California under CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data and to object to certain processing. Email us to exercise these rights.

## Children

The site is not directed at children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their data.
